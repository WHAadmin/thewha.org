Korea 2026 Rates and Registration

CONFERENCE REGISTRATION

You can for our 2026 Conference in Incheon, Korea through our membership database. If you are a WHA member, please log-in to your membership profile before registering in order to get the discounted registration price. If you are registering as a non-member, click on "Continue as Guest" when prompted to log-in.

Registration to the conference includes:

• Entry to all Conference Panels
• Access to the conference App, online forums, and Hybrid sessions
• Entry to Exhibits
• Attendance at Keynote Addresses and Plenary Sessions
• Opening and Closing Receptions
• Conference Materials
• Morning and Afternoon Beverage Breaks

REGISTRATION DATES

Early Registration runs December 15, 2025 through April 15, 2026
Late Registration begins on April 16, 2026
